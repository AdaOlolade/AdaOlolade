01 — ABOUT

Controls that hold up under scrutiny.

I help regulated organizations prove that their technology is secure, governed and audit-ready.

As a Senior IT Auditor, I execute end-to-end integrated audits that evaluate IT general controls, application controls and IT-dependent business processes, from risk-based planning and walkthroughs through design and operating effectiveness testing, issue validation and reporting.

My work spans AWS and Azure cloud governance, cloud-hosted database security, identity and access management, and third-party risk. I am known for clear audit documentation, practical remediation recommendations, and strong partnership with control owners and senior management.

Alongside my audit career I serve in the U.S. Army Reserve and am completing a Master of Science in Information Systems with a cybersecurity concentration.
